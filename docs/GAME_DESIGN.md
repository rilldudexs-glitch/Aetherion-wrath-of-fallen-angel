# AETHERION: Wrath of the Fallen
## Core direction

A compact, story-driven third-person dark-fantasy action game for Android, built with Godot 4.

### Design pillars
- Combat-forward progression with readable enemy patterns.
- A focused campaign across seven distinct regions.
- Soul Arms: a transformable ancient weapon with forms unlocked through the story.
- Progression through weapon and skill upgrades; no character stat allocation.
- Boss encounters built around distinct phases, tells, counters, and arena mechanics.

### Player character
Draeven, a former elite knight of Eldoria, seeks answers after the Night of Ashes destroyed his home and family.

### Core companion
Lyra is an AI-controlled companion who supports selected encounters without replacing Draeven as the central playable character.

### Technical target
- Godot 4, Compatibility renderer.
- Android-first performance budget.
- Initial target: stable 30 FPS on modest hardware.
- Placeholder geometry is temporary and must be replaced by optimized art.

### Current implementation status
Only the project foundation and movement test scene are in scope for this first scaffold. Combat, enemies, story missions, UI, save/load, and Android touch controls are not implemented yet.
